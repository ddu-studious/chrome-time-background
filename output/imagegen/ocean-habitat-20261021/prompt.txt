Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: concept artwork for a browser new-tab ocean habitat, landscape 16:9.
Primary request: Design a believable, quiet living tropical ocean scene that could inspire a more realistic aquarium extension.
Scene/backdrop: A single coherent underwater viewpoint at shallow reef depth, looking horizontally through clear seawater. The rippling underside of the sea surface is high above; pale sand and reef outcrops recede into blue water. No beach or above-water shore is visible.
Subject: A small group of natural reef fish, including a few blue tangs and orange anthias, occupies the middle distance. One sea turtle glides near a side reef. A pair of small clownfish stays close to a sea anemone on the foreground reef. A very distant whale shark silhouette passes through the open blue water beyond the reef. All animals have plausible anatomy, relative scale, spacing and natural colours; they do not pose for the camera.
Composition/framing: Wide desktop wallpaper. Leave the central upper-middle 45 percent as uncluttered blue open water for a future clock and interface, but DO NOT draw any interface or text. Asymmetric reef framing in lower corners, restrained coral variety, subtle foreground occlusion, distinct near, middle and far depth. Wildlife remains secondary to the calm sense of place.
Lighting/mood: Physically plausible sunlight from above-left, soft refracted caustics strongest on shallow sand and nearby rock, warm colours progressively absorbed in the distance, gentle water haze, only sparse tiny suspended particles. Natural underwater photography, realistic matte wet surfaces, delicate fish fins, no fantasy glow.
Constraints: One underwater world and one consistent camera perspective. No freshwater fish, no aquarium glass, no buildings, no divers, no decorative bubbles rising from every animal, no text, numbers, labels, logos or watermark. Avoid neon colours, bloom, lens flare, excessive sharpness, crowded wildlife, plastic-looking fish and exaggerated god rays.
